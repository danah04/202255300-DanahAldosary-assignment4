About

I am a Software Engineering undergraduate at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM), graduating in June 2027.

At the BRAIN Lab, I investigate reasoning in language and vision-language models, with a focus on smaller models and realistic computational constraints. My work asks whether a model is responding to evidence or simply producing a convincing pattern, and what happens when perception itself is wrong.

I am also a member of KFUPM's Emerging Professor Program, which supports my doctoral study and my goal of returning to research and teaching as a faculty member.

January 2026 – Present

Undergraduate Researcher

BRAIN Lab · KFUPM · Supervisor: Dr. Muzammil Behzad

  • Evaluated chain-of-thought prompting and self-consistency across small language and vision-language models to test whether inference-time scaling improves reasoning accuracy.
  • Implemented the evaluation pipeline, ran experiments across multiple models and benchmarks, logged individual reasoning traces, and analyzed accuracy and answer agreement.
  • Found that self-consistency improved the evaluated language models but offered little benefit for the vision-language models, despite greater agreement across sampled answers.
  • Extended the work through failure analysis of perception and reasoning errors and developed the resulting source-attribution question into an undergraduate thesis study.
